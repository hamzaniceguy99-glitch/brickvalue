Terms of service

What we provide

Brickvalue provides the purchase and sale of real estate on its own account by Brickvalue, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0748.467.044, whose establishment carries the trade in residential and non-residential buildings and land. IMPORTANT — ASBESTOS CERTIFICATE: in the Flemish Region an asbestos certificate is required for the transfer of a building constructed before 2001, and the seller must provide it to the purchaser; the certificate identifies the asbestos-containing material present, its condition and the manner in which it should be managed. THE COMPANY OBTAINS THE CERTIFICATE AND PROVIDES IT BEFORE ANY OFFER IS MADE, rather than at the deed, and draws the purchaser's attention to the findings including where they are unfavourable. RENOVATION OBLIGATION: for residential buildings in the Flemish Region, a purchaser acquiring a dwelling with an energy performance label of E or F is subject to an obligation to renovate the property so as to attain at least label D within five years of the authentic deed. THAT OBLIGATION BINDS THE PURCHASER AND NOT THE SELLER, and the cost of complying with it may be substantial; the company discloses the energy performance label and states expressly whether the renovation obligation will apply, so that the figure forms part of the purchaser's calculation before an offer rather than after. SOIL: where the transfer concerns land, a soil certificate issued by the competent Flemish authority is obtained and provided as the applicable legislation requires, together with any information on obligations arising from the soil status. ELECTRICAL INSTALLATION: the inspection report for the electrical installation of a dwelling is provided to the purchaser, and where a report is unfavourable the company states this expressly together with what compliance will require. PLANNING: the company discloses the planning designation of the property, the permits obtained and any planning infringement of which it is aware, and does not present a property as developable or divisible unless the planning position permits it. ENERGY PERFORMANCE: a valid energy performance certificate is held from the moment a property is offered for sale and its indicators are stated in advertising. THE COMPANY IS NOT AN ESTATE AGENT: it buys and sells property of which it is the owner. The profession of estate agent is regulated in Belgium and requires registration with the professional institute for estate agents, which the company does not hold and does not claim; it does not act as an intermediary for third parties, holds no third-party funds and provides no agency service. Purchasers deal with a principal whose interests differ from their own and are encouraged to rely on their own notary, and where appropriate on a professional registered with the ITAA, for advice. The company provides no investment, tax or legal advice and markets no investment product. NO YIELD, CAPITAL GROWTH OR RESALE VALUE IS GUARANTEED, these depending on the market and on conditions outside the company's control. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
